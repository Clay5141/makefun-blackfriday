How these deals were checked

Every price on this page was read on Nov 27, 2026 from the deals source named on each deal — a publication that names the store — and cross-checked the same day. Each card shows the sale price, the usual (list) price, the discount computed from those two prices, the store, and a link to the store. Links open the store's search for the exact product name, so you land on the matching product; no product-page address was ever guessed. Prices can change or sell out — check the store page before you buy.

Verdicts: New low means the lowest price the source has tracked for that item. Strong price means a cut well below the item's usual sale level. Routine sale means a real discount, but one this item sees often, or a small cut on a new product. A routine sale can still be worth buying — it just is not a bargain by itself.
